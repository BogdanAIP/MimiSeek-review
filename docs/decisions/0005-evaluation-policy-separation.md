# ADR 0005 — Evaluation policy is separate from the candidate

## Context

A self-modifying reviewer could appear to improve by weakening the criteria under which it is judged.

## Decision

The candidate reviewer and learner cannot authoritatively modify the evaluation policy governing that candidate's promotion. Evaluation-policy changes are separately governed and cannot retroactively weaken an in-flight candidate evaluation.

## Consequences

- Self-improvement changes reviewer behavior, not the definition of success for the same evaluation.
- Promotion evidence is more resistant to self-confirming loops.
- Policy evolution remains possible, but through a distinct review path.
